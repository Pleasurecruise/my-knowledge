# Product

One personal knowledge library turns completed local writing into durable, retrievable public articles. The web has Home, Articles, Article, and Graph only; there is no owner dashboard. Home owns search, Articles owns chronology, and Graph combines explicit wiki links with shared tags.

Every submission starts public after canonical Chinese persistence completes. The allowed-email owner may create, edit, publish, withdraw, and delete through existing surfaces. Anonymous users receive public keyword/tag search only; AI search requires the owner session. Questions, retrieved context, and generated answers are never stored. Private titles, tags, timestamps, and bodies must never appear in anonymous pages, metadata, social images, feeds, or sitemaps.

Chinese is canonical; supplied English and Japanese editions are optional. Tags are case-insensitive hierarchical paths, with at most five per article and one new leaf per submission. Prefer existing tags. The `daily` hierarchy is excluded from default lists, search, and Graph; explicit tag filters may include it without bypassing visibility.

Multiple owners, comments, revisions, hosted generation, and editorial pipelines are outside scope. [Workflows](WORKFLOWS.md) owns operations; [Content](CONTENT.md) owns document semantics.
